Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

This document was prepared by BASH's founders as a starting privacy policy, based on legal drafting — it has not yet been reviewed by a licensed Kenyan advocate. It will be updated as the platform is formally reviewed.

Obsidian Labs ("Bash", "we", "us")

This Privacy Policy is issued in accordance with the Data Protection Act, 2019 (Laws of Kenya) and the Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021, as amended from time to time. Where any provision of this Policy conflicts with a mandatory requirement of applicable Kenyan data protection law as it stands at the time of processing, the statutory requirement prevails, and this Policy shall be read as automatically adjusted to comply, pending formal update.

1. WHO WE ARE

Bash operates an events discovery, ticketing, and social content platform. For purposes of the Data Protection Act, 2019, Bash is the Data Controller for personal data collected through the Platform.

2. DATA WE COLLECT

Category Examples Purpose
Account data Email, password (hashed), profile info Account creation and authentication
Identity verification National ID or company certificate (Hosts only) Fraud prevention, host eligibility (18+)
Payment data M-Pesa transaction data, bank account number, SWIFT code Processing payments and payouts
Content Photos, videos posted to Cheki Operating the content-sharing feature
Location GPS location (optional) Event discovery/filtering
Usage data Device/app interaction data necessary for security and service functionality Platform security, fraud prevention

We do not collect more data than is necessary for these purposes ("data minimization," per section 25 of the Data Protection Act, 2019).

3. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING

We process personal data on one or more of the following bases, as required by section 30 of the Data Protection Act, 2019: your consent; performance of a contract with you (e.g., processing a ticket purchase); compliance with a legal obligation; or our legitimate interests (e.g., fraud prevention), balanced against your rights.

4. CHILDREN'S DATA (MINOR USERS)

Where a user is between 15 and 17 years of age, we process their personal data only with verifiable parental or guardian consent, obtained via a consent-confirmation email sent to a parent/guardian address provided at signup, in accordance with section 33 of the Data Protection Act, 2019, and applicable regulations on the processing of children's data. Minor Users' access to payment-related and hosting features is restricted (see Terms & Conditions), and full account functionality remains restricted until consent is verified.

5. CROSS-BORDER DATA TRANSFERS

Bash's infrastructure is hosted across multiple jurisdictions, including the European Union and the United States, in addition to Kenya. Where personal data is transferred outside Kenya, Bash ensures such transfers comply with sections 48–50 of the Data Protection Act, 2019 — including, as applicable: transferring only to jurisdictions with adequate data protection safeguards, using appropriate contractual safeguards with our infrastructure providers, or obtaining your consent, as required by the Act and any regulations issued under it from time to time.

6. DATA RETENTION

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, or as required by applicable law (including tax, financial, and Companies Act record-keeping obligations), after which it is securely deleted or anonymized.

6A. SUB-PROCESSORS

We use the following categories of infrastructure providers to operate the Platform, who process personal data on our behalf under contractual safeguards: database hosting (currently provisioned via a provider located in the European Union), application server hosting (currently provisioned via a provider located in the United States), and content delivery/edge network services (globally distributed). We do not sell personal data to third parties.

6B. DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION

In the event of a personal data breach likely to result in risk to your rights, Bash will notify the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of the breach, in accordance with section 43 of the Data Protection Act, 2019, and will notify affected users without undue delay where the breach is likely to result in high risk to their rights and freedoms.

7. YOUR RIGHTS

Subject to the Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to: access your personal data; request correction of inaccurate data; request deletion of your data; object to or restrict certain processing; and data portability, where applicable. To exercise these rights, contact us at Bashevent01@gmail.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) if you believe your data has been mishandled.

8. SECURITY MEASURES

We apply technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the data processed, including access controls restricting sensitive financial data (e.g., bank account details) to authorized systems only.

9. THIS POLICY & CHANGES IN LAW

9.1 This Policy is intended to remain compliant with the Data Protection Act, 2019, and its subsidiary regulations as they may be amended, replaced, or supplemented from time to time. Bash will review and update this Policy to reflect material changes in Kenyan data protection law or in our data practices.

9.2 Where a specific provision of this Policy has not yet been updated to reflect a recent legal change, Bash's obligations under the then-current law apply regardless, and this Policy should not be read as limiting those obligations.
