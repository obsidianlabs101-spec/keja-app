Cookie Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

This document was prepared by BASH's founders based on legal drafting — it has not yet been reviewed by a licensed Kenyan advocate.

Obsidian Labs

1. WHAT COOKIES WE USE

Bash currently uses functional cookies only. We do not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking cookies.

Functional cookies used include those necessary to: - Keep you logged in during your session - Remember your preferences (e.g., language, saved filters) - Support core security functions (e.g., authentication tokens)

2. WHAT WE DO NOT USE

Bash does not currently use: - Analytics cookies (e.g., Google Analytics) - Advertising or retargeting cookies - Third-party social media tracking pixels

Should this change in the future, this Cookie Policy will be updated and, where required by law, your consent will be requested before such cookies are set.

3. LEGAL BASIS

Because Bash's cookies are strictly functional and necessary for the Platform to operate, they fall within the "strictly necessary" exemption under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, and do not require prior opt-in consent — though we disclose them here for transparency. Should Bash later deploy non-essential cookies, opt-in consent will be obtained in accordance with applicable law.

4. MANAGING COOKIES

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling functional cookies may prevent core features (such as staying logged in) from working correctly.

5. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Cookie Policy as the Platform evolves. Material changes will be communicated to users.
